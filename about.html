About Me

Brief Biography

I took a nonlinear path into academia. After teaching for a few years in a joint Chinese–Australian business school, I went to Australia and completed my economics PhD at Uni. of Technology Sydney, within four years. During my PhD, I visited UW–Madison for half a year, interned at the St. Louis Fed, and worked as a research fellow at Uni. of Melbourne.

After working as a Dan Searle Postdoctoral fellow at UW–Madison (2 years) and a permanent lectureship at AUT (1 year, New Zealand), I joined Peking University and have been an Associate Professor of Economics since 2024. During my 2024–2025 sabbatical, I visited New York Uni. and UC Santa Barbara.
