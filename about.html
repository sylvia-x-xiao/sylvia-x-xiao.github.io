About Me

Brief Biography

I took a nonlinear path into academia. After over five years teaching in a joint Chinese–Australian business school, I moved to Australia and completed my economics PhD at the University of Technology Sydney within four years. During my PhD, I spent half a year at UW–Madison, interned at the St. Louis Fed, and worked as a research fellow at the University of Melbourne.

I then held a Dan Searle Postdoctoral Fellowship at the University of Wisconsin–Madison and a permanent lectureship at Auckland University of Technology. I joined Peking University in 2018 and have been an Associate Professor of Economics since 2024. During my 2024–2025 sabbatical, I visited NYU and UC Santa Barbara.
