About Me

Brief Biography

I took a nonlinear path into academia. After completing my master’s degree in my twenties, I spent more than five years as a lecturer in a joint business school run by a Chinese uni. and Uni. of Technology Sydney. I then went to Australia to pursue my PhD in economics there, which I completed within four years. During my PhD, I visited Uni. of Wisconsin–Madison (U.S.) for half a year, interned at St. Louis Fed., and worked as a research fellow at Uni. of Melbourne (Australia).

After graduating, I held a Dan Searle Postdoc Fellowship at UW–Madison (2 years) and a permanent lectureship at Auckland Uni. of Technology (New Zealand), before moving to Peking Uni. in 2018 as an assistant professor. I have been an Associate Professor of Economics at PKU since 2024. I visited NYU and UC-SB during my 2024-2025 sabbatical in the U.S.
